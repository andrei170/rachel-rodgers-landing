FAQ

Common Questions

Who is the Mastermind actually for?

Women founders already running $150K to $2M annual businesses who want to cross seven figures with the right architecture. Coaches, consultants, service founders, boutique agency owners. Not for pre-revenue founders or side-hustlers. The room is built for operators.

What's the investment?

Discussed on the qualification call. The program is a premium commitment in line with the room it places you in. Financing is available for accepted applicants. If the number isn't right for where you are today, we'll point you at The Club instead, which is a lower-entry community.

How long is the commitment?

Membership runs in annual cycles. We build for the long arc, not the quick win. Most members renew at the end of year one because the second year compounds on the first.

What happens on the qualification call?

We walk through your current revenue, your operating model, your goals, and what's genuinely in the way. If the Mastermind is the right room, we talk logistics and get you enrolled. If it isn't, we'll tell you honestly and point you at the right resource instead.

How is this different from other high-ticket masterminds?

Hello Seven is built specifically for women, BIPOC, and LGBTQIA+ founders who are often underpriced, under-supported, and over-delivering. The room reflects that. The architecture addresses it. The mission is built into every decision we make about who we let in and what we teach.
